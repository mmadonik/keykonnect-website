Tour Monitoring

The tour is where the lease is won. Now you can see it.

KeyKonnect records and reviews your in-person showings the same way it reviews your leasing calls, so you know what happened after the door opened.

You can pull a call recording any time you want. Tours are different. Once a prospect walks into the unit, there's no record of what your agent said, what the prospect asked, or whether something was promised that shouldn't have been. Some agents walk every unit like they own the building. Some open the door and wait. Without a record, you can't tell which is which, and you can't coach what you never heard.

How it works

One tap to record. We handle the rest.

Your agents already carry a phone on every tour. That's all it takes.

1
Tap to record
Your agent opens KeyKonnect on their phone, picks the property, confirms they've asked the visitor for permission, and taps record. There's nothing to install.
2
We do the listening
Every tour is transcribed with the agent and the visitor separated, then reviewed against the same coaching and compliance standards we apply to your calls.
3
Your Tour Report arrives weekly
Agent scorecards, coaching notes, concessions mentioned and compliance flags, all in one report in your inbox.
Recording starts with a conversation.

Before every tour, your agent asks the visitor for permission to record and confirms it in the app. Recording can't start until they do, and the confirmation is saved with the tour.

What we listen for

Both sides of every tour.

A transcript tells you what was said. KeyKonnect tells you what it meant for the lease. Here's what we pick up on.

The prospect

What they wanted, what they asked, and how the tour landed with them.

Prospect sentiment

Were they excited, lukewarm or checked out? We track where interest picked up and where it dropped off during the walkthrough.

Prospect questions

Every question the prospect asked gets captured, from parking and pets to utilities and move-in dates. Over time you'll see what people actually want to know about each building.

Stated needs

Commute, schools, budget, space and timing. If a prospect tells your agent what matters to them, it shows up in the report.

Hesitations and concerns

Price, unit size, noise, the building down the street. You'll see what's holding people back before it shows up as a lost lease.

Buying signals

Asking about applications, lease terms or availability dates. These are the moments that tell you a prospect was ready to move forward.

The agent

How your team showed up, and where each person needs coaching.

Engagement

Did the agent lead the tour or just unlock the door? Each agent gets a clear verdict: Strong, Satisfactory, or Needs Improvement.

Building knowledge

How confidently did the agent answer questions about the unit, the building and the neighbourhood? Vague or wrong answers get flagged so you know where training is needed.

Answers and responses

When a prospect asks something, did the agent actually answer it, or move on? We match questions to responses so nothing slips through.

Objection handling

When price or a competing building comes up, you'll see exactly how your agent responded and whether it kept the prospect engaged.

Next steps

Did the tour end with an application, a follow-up or a clear ask? Or did the prospect just walk out the door?

Coaching notes

Specific moments pulled from the tour, covering what went well and what to work on, so feedback isn't based on a gut feeling.

The risk

The things that cost you money or put you in front of a tribunal.

Concessions

Free rent, free parking and waived fees said out loud during a tour get flagged, because that's money leaving the table.

Compliance flags

The same human rights categories we monitor on calls also apply in person, from questions about family status to steering language.
