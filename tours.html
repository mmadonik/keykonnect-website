Tour Intelligence

The tour is where the lease is won. Now you can learn from every one.

KeyKonnect turns your in-person showings into coaching and insight, the same way it does for your leasing calls, so your team knows what's working and what to try next.

Your best agents have a way of showing a unit that just works. Right now, that stays with them. Tours are where prospects make up their minds, but once the visit ends, none of it gets shared: what prospects asked, what they loved, what made them hesitate, and what your strongest people said that turned a maybe into an application. KeyKonnect turns each tour into something your whole team can learn from.

How it works

One tap to start. Insight by the end of the week.

Your agents already carry a phone on every tour. That's all it takes.

1
Start the tour
Your agent opens KeyKonnect on their phone, picks the property, lets the visitor know the tour will be recorded and gets their OK, then taps start. There's nothing to install.
2
We pull out what matters
Each tour is transcribed and summarized, so the useful moments rise to the top without anyone replaying a recording.
3
Your Tour Report arrives weekly
Highlights, coaching ideas for each agent, concessions offered and anything worth a second look, all in one report in your inbox.
Every recording starts with permission.

Before each tour, your agent lets the visitor know and asks if it's okay to record. They confirm it in the app before they can start, and that confirmation is saved with the tour.

What you'll learn

Both sides of every tour.

A transcript tells you what was said. KeyKonnect tells you what it meant for the lease.

The prospect

What they were looking for, what they asked, and how the tour felt to them.

Prospect sentiment

How did the tour land? See which moments sparked interest and where enthusiasm cooled, so you know what resonates in each building.

Prospect questions

The questions prospects ask, from parking and pets to utilities and move-in dates. Over time you'll see what people actually want to know about each building, so your team can answer it before they ask.

Stated needs

Commute, schools, budget, space and timing. A clear picture of what matters most to the people touring your buildings.

Hesitations and concerns

Price, unit size, noise, the building down the street. Understand what gives prospects pause so your team can address it head on.

Buying signals

Questions about applications, lease terms or availability dates. The moments that show a prospect was ready to move forward.

Your team

What's working, where each person shines, and where a little coaching goes a long way.

Tour style

How each agent guides the tour and connects with visitors, with an overall read of Strong, Satisfactory or Needs Improvement to help you focus your coaching.

Building knowledge

How well your team knows the unit, the building and the neighbourhood. Spot where a quick refresher or a better fact sheet would help.

Answers and responses

How prospect questions get answered, so you can see which answers land well and share them across the team.

Objection handling

When price or a competing building comes up, see the responses that keep prospects interested and turn them into talking points everyone can use.

Next steps

How tours wrap up, whether that's an application, a follow-up or a clear plan, so fewer good prospects leave without one.

Coaching notes

Specific moments from each tour, what went well and what to try next time, so feedback is concrete and easy to act on.

The details that matter

The things worth knowing about before they turn into problems.

Concessions

Free rent, free parking or waived fees mentioned during a tour are noted, so you always know what's been offered.

Compliance support

Human rights rules apply in person too. KeyKonnect points out moments worth a second look, like a question about family status, so your team can stay confident and covered.

Learn about the compliance categories
Commitments

A repair, an upgrade, a move-in date. Anything your team offers during a tour is noted so it gets followed through after the visit.

KeyKonnect identifies compliance risks. It does not replace legal counsel.
