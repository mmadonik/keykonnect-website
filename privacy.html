1. Introduction and Scope

17932804 Canada Inc., operating as KeyKonnect ("KeyKonnect", "we", "us" or "our"), provides call and tour analysis software to property management companies, asset managers and asset owners (our "Customers"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal information in connection with:

  • the KeyKonnect application at app.keykonnect.com and related services (the "Services");
  • our website at keykonnect.com (the "Website"); and
  • calls and property tours that our Customers record and analyze using the Services.

By accessing or using the Services or the Website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. Where our Customers have entered into a written agreement with us, that agreement governs in the event of any inconsistency with this Policy.

2. Who We Are and Our Role

KeyKonnect is a Canadian corporation based in Toronto, Ontario.

When our Customers use the Services to record and analyze calls and tours, the Customer determines the purposes for which that information is processed and is responsible for it. In that capacity, KeyKonnect acts as a service provider (or "processor") and handles that information only on the Customer's behalf and in accordance with the Customer's instructions and our agreement with them. Individuals who wish to exercise rights in respect of such information should contact the relevant Customer directly; we will assist our Customers in responding.

KeyKonnect is responsible (as "controller") for personal information we collect for our own purposes, such as account administration, billing, communications with prospective and existing Customers, and Website analytics.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you provide to us directly, including your name, business email address, phone number, job title, organization, account credentials, and the content of communications you send us. Payment information is collected and processed by our payment processor; we do not store full payment card numbers.

3.2 Information Processed Through the Services

On behalf of our Customers, the Services process call and tour recordings, transcripts, call and tour metadata (such as date, time, duration and phone numbers), and analysis generated from that content, including summaries, classifications, performance and compliance indicators, and alerts.

3.3 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Services or visit the Website, we automatically collect technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser and device type, log and session data, and information about how you interact with the Services and the Website.

4. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • provide, operate, maintain and support the Services, including generating analysis, reports and alerts for our Customers;
  • authenticate users and enforce access controls;
  • secure the Services and detect, investigate and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents;
  • maintain and improve the Services;
  • administer accounts, process payments and communicate with Customers about the Services; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Customer recordings or transcripts to train artificial intelligence models without the Customer's express consent, and we do not use personal information processed on behalf of our Customers for advertising.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We collect, use and disclose personal information with consent, or as otherwise permitted or required by applicable law, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy legislation. Where information is processed on behalf of a Customer, the Customer is responsible for establishing the lawful basis for that processing, including any notice or consent required for call and tour recording.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on performance of a contract, our legitimate interests, compliance with legal obligations and, where required, consent.

6. How We Share Information

We disclose personal information only as described in this Policy:

  • Service providers. We engage carefully selected third-party service providers to perform functions on our behalf, including cloud hosting and storage, telephony and call recording, speech-to-text transcription, artificial intelligence analysis, and email delivery. Service providers are permitted to use personal information only to provide services to us, and are bound by written obligations of confidentiality and data protection that provide a comparable level of protection to that set out in this Policy. Information about our service providers is available to Customers on request, subject to confidentiality.
  • Within a Customer's organization. Information in a Customer account is available to that Customer's authorized users in accordance with the permissions set by the Customer.
  • Legal and safety reasons. We may disclose information where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, court order or other legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of KeyKonnect, our Customers or others.
  • Business transactions. Information may be disclosed in connection with an actual or proposed merger, acquisition, financing or sale of all or part of our business, subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations.

7. Where Information Is Stored

Our primary database, which holds account information, transcripts, analysis, metadata and tour recordings, is hosted in Canada.

Certain of our service providers, including those that provide application hosting, telephony and call recording, transcription, artificial intelligence analysis and email delivery, are located in, or process information in, the United States. Personal information may therefore be processed, and in some cases stored, outside Canada, where it may be accessible to the courts, law enforcement and national security authorities of that jurisdiction. We use contractual and other measures to require that such information receives a level of protection comparable to that required under Canadian law.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, to provide the Services, and to meet our legal, accounting and reporting obligations. Unless otherwise agreed with a Customer:

  • recordings, transcripts, analysis and related metadata are retained for the term of the Customer's subscription and for up to 90 days after termination;
  • user account information is retained while the account is active and for up to 30 days after deactivation;
  • technical and usage logs are retained for up to 12 months; and
  • billing records are retained for the period required by applicable tax law.

When information is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it.

9. Data Security

We maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we handle, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication for privileged access, and restriction of access to personal information to authorized personnel with a need to know.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. In the event of a breach of security safeguards involving personal information that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected Customers, individuals and regulators as required by applicable law.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, request that it be corrected, withdraw your consent to certain processing, and request deletion. Depending on where you are located, you may have additional rights, including under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding, and we will respond within the time required by law. If your request concerns information processed on behalf of one of our Customers, we may refer you to that Customer.

You also have the right to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local privacy regulator.

11. Call Recordings

Calls placed to phone numbers provided through the Services may be recorded, and callers are notified of this at the start of the call. Our Customers are responsible for complying with the call and tour recording laws that apply to them. If you have questions about a recording, please contact the property or company you called.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Services use cookies that are strictly necessary for authentication, security and core functionality. We do not use advertising cookies in the Services.

The Website uses analytics and business-identification technologies that collect information such as IP address, pages viewed, referring sites and device type, in order to understand Website traffic and the organizations that visit it. This information may be processed in the United States. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date above indicates when it was last revised. If we make material changes, we will provide notice to Customers by email or through the Services before the changes take effect.
