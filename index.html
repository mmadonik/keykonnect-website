What gets reviewed

Three things you'll know every Monday.

Every call is reviewed the same way, so you can see what's working as clearly as what needs work.

Your team

How each agent handles the fundamentals, with real call examples instead of generalizations.

  • Greeting. Did the call open professionally, with the property and agent named?
  • Qualifying. Did the agent learn what the prospect needs: timing, budget, unit type?
  • Answers. Were questions answered, or left hanging?
  • Objections. When price or a competing building came up, how was it handled?
  • Next step. Did the call end with a tour booked, or at least a clear plan?
  • Contact info. Did the agent confirm how to reach the prospect again?

Your prospects

What people calling about your buildings are asking about, asking for, and objecting to.

  • Most asked questions. What people actually ask about each building, so your team has the answers ready.
  • What's holding people back. Price, unit size, timing, or another building they're looking at.
  • How it's changing. Your monthly PULSE shows how questions, answer rates, and scores move from month to month.

What needs your attention

Flagged for you, so nothing important waits until Monday.

  • Compliance flags under human rights law in every province and territory. Urgent ones are emailed the same day.
  • Missed calls, and whether there's a pattern: lunch hours, Wednesday afternoons, one property.
  • Keyword alerts on the words you choose, sent to you right away.
