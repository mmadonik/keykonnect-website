Common questions

Before you ask.

Do we need to change our phone system?

No. Each property gets a KeyKonnect number that forwards to your agents' existing phones. You point your listings to that number, and nothing else changes.

Do we need to integrate with our property management software?

No. KeyKonnect works on the phone line itself, so there's no integration project and nothing to connect.

Are tours included?

Yes. Tour recording and the weekly Tour Report are included in every plan.

How does compliance monitoring work?

Every call is reviewed against human rights law in every province and territory, and anything urgent is emailed to you the same day. KeyKonnect identifies compliance risks. It does not replace legal counsel.

Can we try it first?

Yes. With a free PULSE Check, we set up one property for two weeks and send you a real PULSE report built from your team's calls. No cost and no commitment.
